A journal of human capacities

- often dismissed, rarely named, always in use.

Tender Ecologies is a cultural publication dedicated to naming and examining the human capacities routinely dismissed as weakness: softness, delicacy, restraint, and other subtle forms of intelligence.

Through essays, visual work, and interviews, it develops language for lived experiences that rarely receive sustained cultural attention.

Issue #1

In Defense of Softness

What if softness is not the opposite of strength, but a form of intelligence in its own right? Moving between desire, motherhood, sexuality, grief, and the politics of the body, this issue explores softness as a force of resistance. An inquiry into the messier, more unruly parts of being human - and what they make possible.

Issue #2

In Defense of Delicacy

We have learned to value what can be measured, scaled, and achieved alone. But what gets lost when individual excellence becomes the measure of everything? Moving through the textures, lures, fantasies, and displacements of food, this issue explores delicacy as a form of human intelligence - revealing the worlds we inherit, the desires that shape us, and what it means to belong.
